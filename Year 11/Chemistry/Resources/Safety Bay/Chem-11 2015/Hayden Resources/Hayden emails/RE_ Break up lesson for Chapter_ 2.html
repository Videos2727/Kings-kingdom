Yesterday:
  • Handed out summary sheet for elements vs compounds vs homogeneous mixtures vs heterogeneous mixtures
  • Completed Set 2.1 Q2 as a class activity and then students shared responses. They felt like they understood it.
  • For Ch 2.2 I only covered briefly and said pure substances have fixed properties but mixtures have properties that can change. Didn't mention physical vs chemical change as I don't believe it's covered in syllabus.
  • I talked through chapter 2.3 and pointed out that a lot of it is similar to Year 8, e.g. filtration. Started writing some brief notes on the board for students to copy. Covered sieving, filtration and vaporisation and got students to include diagrams, pointed out key terminology they may need to learn (e.g. filtrate)

Today:
  • Finished writing notes (simple distillation, fractional distillation, separating funnel). Very briefly mentioned magnetism but didn't discuss it in detail or write any notes.
  • Showed set up distillation apparatus (using copper sulfate solution)
  • Demonstrated use of a separating funnel with water and oil and gave students the chance to either practice with a separating funnel or work on practice questions for upcoming Test #2 (most students chose to start the practice test)
  • In last 20 minutes read out investigation info for separating CuCl2 from NaCl. Got students to think about how they could do separation. They were really unconfident so had to scaffold a lot. Ended up getting to the idea of dissolve in minimal amount of ethanol, filter and then leave ethanol to evaporate. Explained that I didn't want to let them have ethanol near Bunsen burner flames so couldn't boil off ethanol or use distillation. For HW they had to write up the aim, materials and method in their lab books.
