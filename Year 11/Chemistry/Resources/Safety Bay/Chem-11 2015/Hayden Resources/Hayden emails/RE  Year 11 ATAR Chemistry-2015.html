I'm trying the attached method for getting students to learn ions -- breaking up the task and grouping them by pattern. Will give little quizzes at the start of each new week and Test 1 will include naming & writing formula using ions from the Week 1 and Week 2 lists.

I am seeing them for the first time today but after going through introductions and course outline I will hand this out. I'll also review how to write formulae and get them to do Set 13 from Exploring Chemistry Stage 2 (looking up ions and formulae as needed).

Depending on how things go today, next lesson will probably start Chapter 5 of Exploring Chem.

I put ions and moles at the start as this section requires a lot of work and I am hoping that some students will realise quickly that it's not for them and will change course (as opposed to realising that in 6 months time after they failed the exam).
